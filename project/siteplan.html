Horizon Travel Co.

Name: Horizon Travel Co.. I chose this name because it’s simple and professional. I also felt that you could make a cool logo out of it.

The purpose of this website will be to help to plan travel by providing information and various locations with an emphasis on traveling tips and pointers.

Scenarios: Where are the best places to travel for this time of year? What do I need to know before traveling to a specific destination?

Colors:

#fd5f7e Primarily an accent. Borders, underlines, etc.
#001c55 Darker color to add visual emphasis to elements if needed.
#fff0f3 off-white, potentially to be used for the backgrounds.

Fonts:
Oswald for titles and headings.
Clarity City and blocks of text.
